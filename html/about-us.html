Our Story

Experts in Glass Craftsmanship Since 2020

Firozabad Mart is a trusted manufacturer, wholesaler, supplier, and exporter of premium glass products in India, proudly based in Firozabad, the city famously known for its rich glass industry heritage. Established in 2020, we specialize in serving B2B clients across India, including distributors, retailers, wholesalers, interior designers, and project buyers.

Our product range includes crystal chandeliers, glass table lamps, glass hanging lights, glass bangles, and drinkware glasses, designed with superior craftsmanship, durability, and elegant finishing.

Mission

To provide high-quality glass products across India while maintaining competitive pricing, consistent quality, and reliable delivery. To build long-term partnerships with our clients by consistently delivering value, reliability, and customer satisfaction.

Vision

To become a leading global B2B glass product supplier known for innovation, craftsmanship, and trust. To expand our reach worldwide while showcasing the craftsmanship and excellence of Indian glass products.
